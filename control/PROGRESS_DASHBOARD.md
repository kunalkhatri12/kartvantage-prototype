## Update contract

After each audited package or material control decision, update the relevant workbook tabs:

- Dashboard
- Roadmap
- Agent Runs
- Decisions
- Architecture
- Shopify Evidence
- Risks & Blocks
- Change Log
- Source Sheets

Only audited outcomes are promoted to the dashboard. Raw model transcripts remain local under ignored `.agent/runs/`. Do not place secrets, credentials, customer data, merchant tokens, or production identifiers in the workbook.
